01 / The Problem
02 — 11

Medicine mismanagement is
a silent, three-part crisis.

Three failures, one supply chain. Each one compounds the next.
01
CRISIS — 01

Counterfeit & substandard drugs flow unchecked.

Spurious, falsely labelled and substandard medicines enter the supply chain at distribution choke-points — and the patient has no way to verify what they hold before swallowing it.

20%
of India's drug supply is estimated substandard or counterfeit
02
CRISIS — 02

Patients forget, double-dose, or abandon courses.

Without structured reminders, adherence collapses — chronic patients miss doses, others overdose, and antibiotic courses get abandoned mid-way, seeding resistance.

50%
of chronic-disease patients in India fail to adhere to prescribed regimens
03
CRISIS — 03

Expired and unused pills poison the ecosystem.

Households flush or bin expired medicines. Active pharmaceutical ingredients leach into soil and water, while opioids and antibiotics re-enter circulation through informal channels.

₹1,200 Cr
of household medicine wasted annually in India — none of it formally recovered
02 / Why It Matters
03 — 11

These are not isolated problems — they are one broken loop, repeated across 1.4 billion patients, every single day.

A patient buys a strip they cannot verify. They forget a dose, double the next one, or stop early. The leftover strip then sits in a drawer until it expires — and is then thrown into a bin, into a drain, or sold onward.

Today, no actor in the system sees the full loop. Regulators see only post-market surveillance data. Pharmacists see only the sale. Patients see only the strip in their hand.

MedScan+ connects all three on a single software layer — without disrupting the existing pharmaceutical chain.

04 / How It Works — Part 1
05 — 11

Find the medicine,
then verify it.

01

Open the app. Point at the strip.

The scanner reads the QR / datamatrix on the pack — falling back to OCR for older strips without a code.

CV · QR · OCR · ON-DEVICE
02

Hash matched against regulator DB.

The serialised code is verified against CDSCO / state FDA serial-number registries via a signed API call.

API · CDSCO · SIGNED
03

Patient sees verified medicine card.

Brand, batch, manufacture & expiry date, manufacturer name, and a green/red authenticity verdict.

UI · GREEN / RED VERDICT
04

If fake — alert is auto-filed.

Geo-tagged report pushed to the state FDA's counterfeit-drug desk for follow-up at the source pharmacy.
