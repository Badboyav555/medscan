01 / The Problem
02 — 11

Medicine mismanagement is
a silent, three-part crisis.

Three failures, one broken patient journey.
01
CRISIS — 01

Wrong Medicine Errors

Prescriptions are unclear or the wrong strip is picked up at the pharmacy — and there is no system to cross-check it before consumption.

02
CRISIS — 02

Massive Wastage

Most households discard unused medicine. It isn’t tracked, returned, or reused safely — ending up in drains and dustbins.

03
CRISIS — 03

Unaffordable Access

Families don't know where the same medicine is available for free (Govt/MMNDY) or at a fraction of the price (Jan Aushadhi) nearby.

03 / The Solution
04 — 11

One platform,
four connected modules.

MedScan+ follows a medicine’s full journey — from finding it, to safely using it, to responsibly recovering what’s left.
M—01
Find

Locate Nearby

Search a medicine and see the nearest place it’s available free (Govt/MMNDY) or cheapest (Jan Aushadhi, pharmacy).

  • Free → Cheapest → Nearest
  • Live map directions
M—02
Verify

Catch Mismatches

Scan prescription + medicine packet. App flags any mismatch in name, strength, or dosage before you take it.

  • OCR extraction
  • Instant mismatch alert
M—03
Manage

Auto-Schedule

Auto-built schedule from the verified prescription — reminders, taken/missed tracking, and expiry alerts.

  • Zero manual entry
  • Family mode included
M—04
Recover

Safe Returns

Unused, sealed medicine routed to an authorized collection point — verified reuse or safe scientific disposal.

  • Eligibility check
  • QR verified drop-off
