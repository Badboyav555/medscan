The Problem

Medicine mismanagement is a silent, three-part crisis

Wrong Medicine Errors

Prescription is unclear or the wrong strip is picked up at the pharmacy — no one cross-checks it.

Massive Wastage

Most households discard unused medicine — it isn't tracked, returned, or reused safely.

Unaffordable Access

Families don't know where the same medicine is available free or at a fraction of the price nearby.

The gap: No single system today connects what a patient is prescribed, what they actually take, and what happens to the medicine that's left over.

The Solution

One platform, four connected modules

MedScan+ follows a medicine's full journey — from finding it, to safely using it, to responsibly recovering what's left.

1. FIND

Search a medicine and see the nearest place it's available free (govt/MMNDY) or cheapest (Jan Aushadhi, pharmacy).

2. VERIFY

Scan prescription + medicine packet. App flags any mismatch in name, strength, or dosage before you take it.

3. MANAGE

Auto-built schedule from the verified prescription — reminders, taken/missed tracking, expiry alerts.

4. RECOVER

Unused, sealed medicine routed to an authorized govt/pharmacy collection point — verified reuse or safe disposal.

How It Works — Part 2

Stay on schedule, then close the loop responsibly

MANAGE — Auto schedule, zero manual entry

Daily Reminders — Notification at the exact dose time set from the verified prescription

Taken / Missed Tracking — One-tap logging builds an adherence history over time

Expiry Alerts — Warns before a medicine in stock expires unused

Family Mode — One account tracks medicines for elderly parents & children too

RECOVER — Legally-safe return, not resale

1

Eligibility check: sealed? unexpired by 3-6 months?

2

Source-based routing: Private → authorized centre; Govt hospital → govt facility; Jan Aushadhi → future integration (pending approval)

3

Nearest collection point shown on map

4

Pharmacist scans & verifies on drop-off (QR) — creates a digital record

5

Eligible → verified reuse/donation; Not eligible → safe scientific disposal
